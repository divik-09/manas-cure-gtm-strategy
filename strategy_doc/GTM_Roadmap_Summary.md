# Go-To-Market (GTM) Expansion Roadmap: Mana's Cure

This framework details the strategy developed to target access-constrained, low-income patient markets for modern clinical distribution solutions.

## 🎯 Target Segment Framing (Jobs-To-Be-Done)
We utilized the **Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)** framework based on primary field data to catalog patient behavior parameters:
* **Core Functional Job:** Accessing affordable, reliable medical guidance without risking a day's wages on travel or diagnostic delays.
* **Emotional Job:** Reducing family anxiety surrounding chronic medical conditions via transparent care channels.

## 🗺️ Phase-Constrained Roadmap Strategy
1. **Phase 1: Local Density Optimization** (Months 1–6): Establish anchor validation footprints inside underserved municipal perimeters using localized micro-channels.
2. **Phase 2: Affordability-First Pricing Adjustments** (Months 7–12): Deploy subscription tiers structured with flexible micro-payment options to lower purchase barriers while maintaining steady unit economics.
3. **Phase 3: Ecosystem Network Integration** (Months 13–24): Scale regional access using low-bandwidth interfaces tailored to rural communication setups.

## 📊 Business Performance & Metric Trajectory
* **Target Growth Run-rate:** 524% Projected YoY Growth Vector.
* **Core Metric Pillars:** Maximizing lifetime value (LTV) while tightly optimizing customer acquisition cost (CAC) through community-anchored networks.
